# 002 — Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope

## 1. Primary Goals
The objective of the CoinPilot AI MVP is to deliver a fully functional crypto intelligence web-app inside Telegram, providing high-value market signal summaries while validating user retention through an energy economy.

---

## 2. In-Scope Features

### Feature A: Coin Intelligence Cards
- **Assets:** BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT (Futures & Spot).
- **Consensus Engine Output:** Consolidated Score (0–100), Direction (LONG / SHORT / NEUTRAL), and ATR-based Trade Setup (Entry, Stop-Loss, Take-Profit targets).
- **AI Explanation:** 2–3 sentence institutional rationale generated via LLM.

### Feature B: Portfolio AI (Basic)
- Manual entry of held assets (Symbol, Quantity, Buy Price).
- Total portfolio valuation in USDT.
- Instant AI health score based on asset concentration and trend alignment.

### Feature C: Freemium Energy & Referrals
- **Daily Energy:** 100 energy units renewed every 24 hours.
- **Consumption:** 10 energy units per Coin Intelligence Card query.
- **Referral Loop:** Inviter receives +50 bonus energy per onboarded user.

---

## 3. Out-of-Scope (Deferred to v2.0)
- Automated API exchange trading / Execution bots.
- On-chain wallet analytics.
- Real-time WebSocket push notifications for liquidation alerts.
- Paid Telegram Stars/TON subscription billing.
