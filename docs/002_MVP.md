# 002 — Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope — v2 (Revised)

## 0. Why This Revision

The original MVP scope centered on a directional Consensus Score (LONG/SHORT/NEUTRAL) with explicit entry/SL/TP levels. That approach carries three structural risks:

1. **Unvalidated edge.** Factor weights (30/25/20/15/10%) are not backed by historical backtesting, so the score's predictive value is unproven.
2. **Regulatory/liability exposure.** Explicit trade setups read as financial advice in most jurisdictions.
3. **Retention fragility.** Value collapses the moment a signal is wrong — a common failure mode for crypto-signal Telegram bots.

This revision keeps the same tech stack and data sources, but repositions the product core around utility that doesn't depend on being "right" about market direction.

---

## 1. Primary Goals

Deliver a crypto intelligence web-app inside Telegram that helps users understand their portfolio risk and market context clearly, validating retention through genuinely useful (not merely predictive) daily-use features.

---

## 2. In-Scope Features

### Feature A: Portfolio Risk Intelligence (Core)

- Manual entry of held assets (Symbol, Quantity, Buy Price, Side: Spot/Futures).
- Total portfolio valuation in USDT, updated live.
- **AI Risk Analysis:**
  - Concentration risk (% exposure per asset/sector).
  - Directional bias (net long/short exposure across portfolio).
  - Correlation warning (e.g., "70% of your holdings move together").
  - Scenario stress-test: "If BTC drops 10%, your portfolio value changes by X%."
- This becomes the primary daily-use surface — not a secondary feature.

### Feature B: Market Context Cards (Non-Directional)

- **Assets:** BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT (Futures & Spot).
- Same underlying data as the original Consensus Engine (trend, order flow, derivatives, liquidations, sentiment) — but presented as a **descriptive summary**, not a directional call or score.
- AI Explanation: 2–3 sentence factual context (e.g., "Funding rates are elevated and open interest is rising, suggesting crowded long positioning near current levels.").
- No explicit LONG/SHORT recommendation, no entry/SL/TP targets in v2.
- Directional Consensus Score is deferred to v2.0+ and only shipped after a documented backtest (see Section 4).

### Feature C: Smart Alerts (Informational)

- User-configured alerts on objective, easily-verifiable conditions: RSI divergence, funding rate spikes, OI surges, liquidation cluster proximity.
- Framed strictly as "notable market events," not trade signals.
- Alert accuracy/frequency is trackable and can be shown transparently to the user over time.

### Feature D: Freemium Energy & Referrals

- **Daily Energy:** 100 energy units renewed every 24 hours.
- **Consumption:** 10 energy units per Market Context Card or Portfolio Risk refresh.
- **Referral Loop:** Inviter receives +50 bonus energy per onboarded user.
- **Anti-abuse:** Referral bonus requires referred user to reach a minimum activity threshold (e.g., 3 portfolio queries) before bonus is credited, to reduce multi-account farming.

---

## 3. Out-of-Scope (Deferred to v2.0+)

- Directional Consensus Score with explicit LONG/SHORT/NEUTRAL call and ATR-based trade setups — gated behind a published backtest (win-rate, drawdown, sample size) before release.
- Automated API exchange trading / execution bots.
- On-chain wallet analytics.
- Real-time WebSocket push notifications.
- Paid Telegram Stars/TON subscription billing.
- Trade journal with AI post-hoc pattern review (strong v2 candidate — flagged for prioritization after core retention is validated).

---

## 4. Validation Gate for Directional Scoring (New)

Before any directional score or trade-setup feature ships, produce:

- A backtest of the proposed factor weights against historical price action (minimum 6–12 months, multiple market regimes).
- Reported metrics: win-rate by direction, average R:R achieved, max drawdown, sample size per asset.
- These metrics displayed transparently in-app if the feature ships, so users see track record rather than a bare score.

---

## 5. Success Metrics for MVP

- D7/D30 retention of Portfolio Risk feature (primary signal of real utility, independent of market outcomes).
- Alert opt-in and repeat-usage rate.
- Referral conversion rate post anti-abuse threshold.
- Energy consumption pattern (are users returning daily, or burning energy once and churning?).
